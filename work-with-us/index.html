Client work · Udokk · Health

The Dokkcast: expert health conversations people choose to hear

The challenge
Udokk wanted to share trustworthy health information from real experts, in a format people would choose, and make sure it reached them.
What we did
Launched The Dokkcast: recording, editing, distribution on Apple Podcasts, Spotify and YouTube, episode artwork, the show’s website, and clips and posts from every episode.
The result
14 episodes and counting with guests including Professor John Deanfield CBE, and a steady flow of posts without Udokk’s team finding the time.

Questions

The things people ask before they start

Anything else, just ask on the call.

We’re not sure what we need. Where do we start?

Book a free 20-minute podcast chat. Tell us about your show, or the idea for one, and we’ll suggest the right mix with a clear price. No obligation.

We already record. Can you just do part of it?

Yes. Plenty of people only want editing, distribution, shorts or YouTube growth. Pick one service or all six.

How much of our time will it take?

Mostly the recording itself. We handle the planning, editing, publishing and posting, and only need your approval before anything goes out.

Who will we work with?

Damien and Peter Bidmead. MBMS Podcast Studio is the two of us, so you work directly with the people making your show. Meet us.

Can you come to us?

Yes. We bring our RØDE mobile studio to your office, event or chosen venue, record at Great Portland Street Studio in London, or record guests remotely with Riverside.

How will we know it’s working?

We look at downloads, views, subscribers and the enquiries your show brings in, and use what we learn to shape the next episodes.
