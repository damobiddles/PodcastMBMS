Questions

The things people ask before they start

Anything else, just ask on the call.

We’re busy. How much of our time will this take?

Very little. You join a planning call, turn up to record and approve the edit. We handle the preparation, production, clips and posts. See the time breakdown above.

What if we run out of things to say?

Most podcasts stall around episode three because nobody planned beyond the first few ideas. We plan each episode around a goal, keep a running list of topics and help you line up guests, such as partners, customers and experts from your own network, so the show keeps going.

How will we know it’s working?

We agree a few simple measures at the start, such as listens, clip views and clicks to a landing page or booking form, and check them as the episodes go out. We’d recommend giving a podcast at least six months before judging it.

We don’t have a studio. Where do we record?

At our Great Portland Street Studio in London, at your office or venue with our mobile kit, or remotely with guests joining from a browser link. Many good episodes are recorded in an ordinary meeting room.

Who would we even invite?

Usually more people than you think: partners, customers, suppliers, people in your industry you admire, and your own team. We help you build the guest list in the planning call.

Is a podcast really worth it for a business our size?

If it’s just an episode posted once, probably not. Planned properly, one recording gives you a month of clips, posts and articles, so you’re paying for a content engine rather than a single piece. That’s the difference we focus on.
