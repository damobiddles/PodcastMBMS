Questions

The things people ask before they start

Anything else, just ask on the call.

We’re not sure what we need. Where do we start?

Book a free 20-minute marketing check. We’ll look at where you are and suggest one or two things worth doing next, with no obligation. You can also try our brand health check first.

We’re busy. How much of our time will this take?

Less than you might think. We need a first call, a conversation we can build on and your approval before anything goes out. We handle the planning, the making and the follow-up.

Do we need a podcast to work with you?

No. Podcasts and video are one of the ways we make content, not a requirement. Many projects start from an interview or a workshop instead.

Who will we work with?

Your first conversation is with the person who will lead your project, not a salesperson. When a project needs extra skills, we bring in trusted specialists in design, web development, paid ads, PR and media relations, video and photography, and copywriting. Meet the team.

How will we know it’s working?

We agree a few measures that matter to your business at the start, such as enquiries, sign-ups or sales conversations, and check them as the work goes out. Then we use what we learn to plan the next round.

Do you only work with businesses near Marlow?

No. We’re based in Marlow, Buckinghamshire, and work with businesses in London and beyond. Most of the work happens on calls and online, and when we record, we can use Great Portland Street Studio in London, come to your office or record remotely.
