Illustrative plan · B2B SaaS

What we'd do for a B2B software company launching a new product

The brief

A software company with a small sales team is launching a new product to IT and operations leaders. Their website talks about features, not problems, and the sales team writes its own decks. They want qualified sales conversations, not just traffic.

How one conversation becomes content

  • One customer conversation
  • Launch page copy
  • Buyer's guide
  • Founder LinkedIn posts
  • Short clips
  • Sales email follow-ups
  • Search topics

The plan

  1. Weeks 1 to 3: Listen and shapeInterview the founder, two salespeople and a handful of customers. Turn what we hear into a short positioning statement, three key messages and a launch page brief.
  2. Weeks 4 to 8: LaunchRecord a short podcast or video series with the product team and early customers. Publish the launch page, a guide that answers the buyer's main question, LinkedIn posts from the founder, and an email sequence for existing leads.
  3. Weeks 9 to 12: Learn and adjustLook at which messages and channels brought real conversations, cut what didn't work and plan the next quarter around what did.

What we'd measure

  • Demo or sales-call requests from the launch page
  • Which messages sales say land in calls
  • Sign-ups for the guide, and how many become conversations
  • Time from first visit to first call

Illustrative plan · Local professional services

What we'd do for a local accountancy or law firm

The brief

A well-regarded firm in the Thames Valley gets most of its work by word of mouth. The partners are known locally, but the website is out of date and nobody has time to post. They want a steadier flow of the right kind of enquiry.

How one conversation becomes content

  • One partner Q&A
  • Service page
  • Article
  • LinkedIn post
  • Short clip
  • Local newsletter
  • Google Business Profile update

The plan

  1. Month 1: Listen and shapeA planning session with the partners to agree the two or three services they most want to grow. Rewrite the website pages for those services in plain English, with a clear way to get in touch.
  2. Months 2 and 3: ShareRecord short conversations with the partners answering the questions clients ask most. Turn each one into a website article, a LinkedIn post, a short clip and a local newsletter. Tidy the Google Business Profile and ask happy clients for reviews.
  3. Ongoing: LearnA short monthly note on enquiries by service and source, and which articles people read before getting in touch.

What we'd measure

  • Enquiries by service, and where they came from
  • Calls and form fills from the website
  • Searches that find the firm locally
  • Reviews, and how quickly enquiries get a reply

Illustrative plan · Healthcare

What we'd do for a healthcare brand building trust

The brief

A healthcare company has good evidence behind its product but struggles to explain it simply. Its audience is wary of hype, and every claim has to be checked. They want to be the source people trust, without overpromising.

How one conversation becomes content

  • One expert conversation
  • Plain-English article
  • Quote cards
  • Short clips
  • Newsletter
  • FAQ page
  • Press angles

The plan

  1. Month 1: Listen and shapeAgree the audience, the questions they ask and the claims the company can stand behind. Set up a simple review step so clinical and compliance colleagues can sign off content quickly.
  2. Months 2 to 4: ShareAn expert-led podcast or video series where clinicians explain one topic per episode in plain language. Each episode becomes an article, quote cards, short clips and a newsletter, all checked before they go out.
  3. Ongoing: LearnTrack which topics people search for and come back to, and use that to plan the next series.

What we'd measure

  • Return visitors and newsletter sign-ups
  • Which topics people search for and read in full
  • Questions and enquiries that mention the content
  • Time from draft to approved content
