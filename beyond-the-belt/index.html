Our mission

A belt shows what you have learned. We care about who you become while you learn it.

Martial arts teach far more than technique. Turning up when it is hard, losing and trying again, trusting a training partner and being trusted back: those lessons stay with you long after the grading.

Beyond the Belt exists so that every young person who could benefit from that experience can have it, whatever their circumstances or whether their family can afford the fees.

Our sessions are led by experienced, safeguarding-trained coaches in the welcoming environment of the academy that started it all.

What we do

Training with a purpose

Every programme is built around the same idea: use the discipline and community of martial arts to help young people thrive.

01

Free & supported places

Funded places in regular classes, including kit and gradings, for young people who would otherwise miss out.

02

Confidence & resilience

Mentoring woven into training, helping young people handle pressure, setbacks and big emotions in a healthy way.

03

Workshops & outreach

Taster sessions and anti-bullying and personal-safety workshops for schools, youth groups and community partners.

04

Community days

Open sessions, family events and fundraisers that bring the whole neighbourhood onto the mats.

The pathway

From first class to giving back

Progress is not only measured in stripes. We support each young person along a path that ends with them helping the next one through the door.

  1. Show up

    A warm welcome, no experience needed and no pressure. The hardest step is walking in.

  2. Learn

    Technique, discipline and teamwork, at a pace that suits the individual.

  3. Lead

    Taking responsibility: helping with warm-ups, mentoring newcomers, setting an example.

  4. Give back

    Volunteering, coaching pathways and community projects that put their skills to work.

Our story

Born at the academy

Beyond the Belt grew out of a martial arts academy and the community that has built up around it over the years: coaches, students, parents and friends who have seen what time on the mats can do for a young person.

The charity lets us take that further, opening the door to people who need it most and backing it with proper safeguarding, structure and care.

Two figures, one belt, and a path that leads well beyond the dojo.
