Brand colours

Fonts

Fonts

Poppins ExtraBold

For headlines, the logo and label boxes. Use ExtraBold (800) for headlines and Bold (700) for buttons and subheads. Free from Google Fonts.

Inter

For body text, captions and everything else. Regular (400) for reading, SemiBold (600) for labels and service lists. Free from Google Fonts.

Type rules

  • Headlines in sentence case, tightly spaced, ending with a full stop when they are a sentence
  • Body text at least 16px on screen, with comfortable line spacing
  • Small labels above headings in deep gold Inter SemiBold

Avoid

  • Headlines in ALL CAPS (MBMS is the only exception)
  • Any other fonts, or Poppins for long paragraphs
  • Italic or light weights for headlines

Graphics

Label boxes and graphic elements

Our look is built from four simple pieces. Put them together the same way every time and anything we make looks like MBMS, even without the logo.

Please do

  • Keep outlines solid ink, about 1.5px on screen and 3px on banners and print
  • Leave plenty of white or stone space around label boxes
  • Use a stone shape or a single dot to fill empty space

Please don’t

  • Round the corners of label boxes or add drop shadows to them
  • Use more than one sunflower box in a headline
  • Tilt anything more than 2°, or set boxes on busy photos

Voice and naming

How we write and what we call things

Our name

  • First mention: Meadow Brand Marketing Services (MBMS). After that, MBMS
  • The logo says MBMS, always in capitals
  • We are a boutique marketing firm with content at the core
  • Always written MBMS: never “MBMS Podcast Studio”, “Meadow BMS” or “M.B.M.S.”

Our six services

  • Brand strategy & identity
  • Digital & performance
  • Social media & community
  • Creative & web design
  • PR & influencer outreach
  • Data & growth analysis

Short form, in this order: Brand · Digital · Social · Web · PR · Growth.

How we sound

  • “We”, not “I” or “MBMS is”. We talk to “you”
  • Plain, warm and direct. Short sentences, everyday words
  • UK English: colour, organise, programme
  • One clear next step. Our main button says “Book a call”

What we never do

  • Make up results, numbers, quotes or client names
  • Call past employers our clients: they are past roles
  • Use jargon like “synergy”, “leverage” or “360° solutions”
  • Overclaim: we say “more than 15 years” of marketing experience, no more

Lines we use

  • Headline: One good conversation, turned into all your marketing.
  • Descriptor: Boutique marketing with content at the core.
  • Location: Based in Marlow, working in London and beyond.

About us

Company description

Copy and paste whichever length you need.

Short

MBMS (Meadow Brand Marketing Services) is a boutique marketing firm based in Marlow, with content at the core. We help growing businesses with brand, digital, social, web, PR and growth, all powered by content made from real conversations: podcasts, video, clips and articles.

Long

MBMS (Meadow Brand Marketing Services) is a boutique marketing firm based in Marlow, Buckinghamshire, working in London and beyond. We help growing businesses with brand strategy and identity, digital and performance marketing, social media, creative and web design, PR and influencer outreach, and data and growth analysis. Content runs through all of it: we record conversations at Great Portland Street Studio in London, at clients’ offices and remotely, then turn them into clips, posts, articles and campaigns. Our experience spans B2B SaaS, cybersecurity, insurance technology, healthcare, retail and the video games industry. We also make The Podcast Pivot, a show for businesses that want to podcast properly.
