About us

Company description

Copy and paste whichever length you need.

Short

MBMS Podcast Studio is a podcast studio with a marketing background, based in Marlow and recording in London and on location. We plan, record and produce podcasts for businesses, then turn every episode into clips and content that reach the right people.

Long

MBMS Podcast Studio is a podcast studio with a marketing background, based in Marlow, Buckinghamshire. We record at Great Portland Street Studio in London, at clients’ offices and venues with our mobile studio, and remotely with guests anywhere in the world. Our experience spans B2B SaaS, cybersecurity, insurance technology, healthcare, retail and the video games industry, so we don’t just record and edit: we help businesses shape the idea, plan the guests and turn every conversation into clips, posts and articles that support their goals. We also make The Podcast Pivot, a show for businesses that want to podcast properly.
