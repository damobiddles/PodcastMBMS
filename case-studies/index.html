The Dokkcast for Udokk

Client work · Udokk · Health

The Dokkcast: expert health conversations people choose to hear

The challenge
Udokk wanted to share trustworthy health information from real experts, in a format people would choose, and make sure it reached them.
What we did
Launched The Dokkcast: recording, editing, distribution on Apple Podcasts, Spotify and YouTube, episode artwork, the show’s website, and clips and posts from every episode.
The result
14 episodes and counting with guests including Professor John Deanfield CBE, and a steady flow of posts without Udokk’s team finding the time.

Our own tools · Offcuts and Post Desk

One episode, a month of posts

Offcuts finds the best moments in each episode and turns them into clips, captions and covers. Post Desk lays out the month so whoever posts can do it in a couple of taps. A person checks everything before it goes out.
