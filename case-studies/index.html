Udokk · Health · Content, web and social

Making expert health content people choose to hear

The challenge
Udokk wanted to share trustworthy health information from real experts, in a format people would choose, and make sure it reached them.
What we did
Launched The Dokkcast: production, its own website, episode artwork, and clips, articles and social posts from every episode.
The result
14 episodes and counting with guests including Professor John Deanfield CBE, a website Udokk owns, and a steady flow of posts without their team finding the time.

Giving a local builder a website that wins work

A Buckinghamshire builder’s website was stuck on a closed site builder, hard to change and awkward on phones.

Moved it off Duda onto hosting we manage, then clearer headings and type, better search basics and layouts that work on any screen.

A site that shows off their best work, led by a photo gallery for the Castle Hill new build, and that we can update quickly whenever they need.
