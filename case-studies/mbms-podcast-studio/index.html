How we built our own podcast, website and content engine

Before we ask you to trust us with your podcast and marketing, here’s what we built for ourselves: everything a business needs to be found, look established and turn interest into enquiries, in two and a half weeks.

The brief

A new studio that needed to look established

We had the experience and the clients, but no website, no consistent brand and no way of showing what we could do beyond recording. So we set ourselves the same brief we’d give any client.

What we did

Website

mbmspodcaststudio.com, built from scratch: services and prices, case studies, enquiry forms that go straight to an inbox or CRM, and set up to be found in search and AI tools.

Content

Sixteen articles answering questions buyers already ask, each with a LinkedIn share image and a link on to services or a conversation.

Brand and creative

A bold “studio zine” look across the site, social channels, podcast artwork and a public media kit of 20 downloadable files.

Podcast, leads and sales

The Podcast Pivot, with show notes, chapters and LinkedIn posts for every episode, plus a free checklist, a local offer and a Work with us page.

Results so far

Everything a new business needs, in place

  • A full website, brand and podcast live in two and a half weeks.
  • A library of content that keeps answering buyers’ questions and linking to our services.
  • Lead capture and sales materials our team uses after every conversation.
