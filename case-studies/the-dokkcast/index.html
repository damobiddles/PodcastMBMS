The Dokkcast: a health podcast with its own website, artwork and social channels

An evidence-led health podcast from Udokk, hosted by Dr Nara Daubeney. Each episode is an open, accessible conversation with a doctor, clinician or specialist. We make the show, built its website and run its content and social media.

The brief

Credible health content, without the hype

Udokk wanted to share trustworthy health information from people who really know their subject, in a format people would actually choose to listen to, then make sure those conversations reached people.

What we did

Production

Multi-camera recording at our London studio and at Udokk’s office, edited and published as video and audio, with episode artwork for every release.

Website

We built thedokkcast.com: episode pages, show notes for every episode, host and guest pages, and a media kit. A home for the show that Udokk owns.

Content

Every episode becomes an article, quote cards and short clips, so one conversation keeps working for weeks.

Social media

Posts written for Instagram and LinkedIn and scheduled ahead, so the show has a steady presence without Udokk’s team finding the time.

Results so far

A growing show with a home of its own

  • 14 episodes and counting, with guests including Professor John Deanfield CBE and Dr Alexander Lyon.
  • A website Udokk owns, with every episode, its show notes and a blog that builds search visibility over time.
  • A steady flow of social content from every episode, written and scheduled for them.
