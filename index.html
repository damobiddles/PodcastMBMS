FAQ

Questions we're often asked

Straight answers on prices, venues and how we work. Anything else, just ask.

How much does it cost to record a podcast in London?

A recording session costs £250 for up to 90 minutes. That includes preparing the studio, a sound check, monitoring the recording and supplying the raw audio files afterwards. Our prices are guidelines, and we're happy to be flexible depending on the work.

How much does podcast editing cost?

Audio editing starts from £175 per episode, and audio and video editing from £350 per episode. Edited transcripts start from £85 per episode, and show notes and episode descriptions from £95 per episode.

Do you offer a complete podcast package?

Yes. A complete package covering recording, editing, show notes, distribution and three social clips starts from around £950 per episode. For regular customers we can build a monthly package based on the number of episodes and campaign assets you need.

Where do you record podcasts?

We record at a choice of venues in London, including Hale House. We can also bring our RØDE mobile studio to your office, event or chosen venue, or record guests remotely.

Can you record podcast guests remotely?

Yes. We record remote guests with Riverside. Guests join from a link in their web browser, and each person is recorded in high quality on their own device, so a patchy internet connection doesn't spoil the recording. Each speaker gets a separate audio and video track for editing.

Can you make short clips from my existing YouTube podcast?

Yes. Send us an episode, as a YouTube link or the original recording files, and we'll choose the strongest moments and turn them into captioned vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn. A clip pack of 5 clips starts from £195 per episode, 10 clips from £345, and monthly clips from £595 a month.

Can you turn podcast episodes into social media content?

Yes. We create short clips for LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, produce supporting social posts and articles, and help align podcast topics with your wider marketing campaigns.

Which platforms can you publish my podcast to?

We handle uploading, scheduling and distribution across the major podcast platforms, including Apple Podcasts, Spotify and YouTube.
