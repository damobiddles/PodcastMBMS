FAQ

Questions we’re often asked

Straight answers on what we do, where we record and what it costs. Anything else, just ask.

What does MBMS Podcast Studio do?

We make, distribute and market podcasts. We plan the show with you, record it in the studio, at your place or remotely, edit and transcribe it, publish it as video and audio on every major platform, turn each episode into a month of shorts, and grow your YouTube channel.

Do I have to use all six services?

No. Pick one or all of them. Some clients just want recording and editing, some already record and only want the shorts or the YouTube work, and some want the whole thing done for them.

Can you come to us?

Yes. We bring our RØDE mobile studio to your office, event or chosen venue in Marlow, the Thames Valley, London or further afield. We also record at Great Portland Street Studio in London, or record guests remotely with Riverside.

How much does a podcast cost?

A recording session costs £250 for up to 90 minutes, and a complete episode package with editing, show notes, distribution and three social clips starts from around £950 per episode. All prices are guidelines. See all our prices and worked examples in our guide to podcast costs.

Which platforms will my podcast be on?

Every major platform, in video and audio: YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music and the other main podcast apps. We upload, schedule and publish each episode for you.

Can you make shorts from my existing YouTube podcast?

Yes. Send us an episode, as a YouTube link or the original recording files, and we'll choose the strongest moments and turn them into captioned vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn. A clip pack of 5 clips starts from £195 per episode.

Who will I work with?

Damien and Peter Bidmead. MBMS Podcast Studio is the two of us, so the people you meet are the people who plan, record, edit and grow your show. Meet us.

How do I get started?

Book a free 20-minute podcast chat. Tell us about your podcast, or the one you’re planning, and we’ll suggest the right mix with a clear price. We reply within two working days.
