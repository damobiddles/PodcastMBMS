FAQ

Questions we're often asked

Straight answers on what we do, how we price it and how we work. Anything else, just ask.

What does MBMS do?

We’re a boutique marketing team based in Marlow. We offer six services: brand strategy and identity, digital and performance marketing, social media and community, creative and web design, PR and influencer outreach, and data and growth analysis. Content runs through all of it, made from real conversations with you: podcasts, video, clips and articles. See all our services.

Do I need a podcast to work with you?

No. You can pick any one of our services or put several together. Podcasts and video are one of the ways we make content, not a requirement.

How do you price your work?

Our marketing services are priced per project. Every business needs a different mix, so we quote for what you actually need. Podcast recording, editing and clips have guide prices on our Services page. See podcast prices.

Who will I work with?

Your first conversation is with the person who will lead your project, not a salesperson. When a project needs extra skills, we bring in trusted specialists in design, web development, paid ads, PR and media relations, video and photography, and copywriting. Meet the team.

Where are you based?

We’re based in Marlow, Buckinghamshire, and work with businesses in London and beyond. When we record, we use Great Portland Street Studio in London, bring our mobile studio to your office, or record guests remotely.

How much does a podcast cost?

A recording session costs £250 for up to 90 minutes, and a complete episode package with editing, show notes, distribution and three social clips starts from around £950 per episode. All prices are guidelines. See worked examples in our guide to podcast costs, or more podcast questions.

Can you make short clips from my existing YouTube podcast?

Yes. Send us an episode, as a YouTube link or the original recording files, and we'll choose the strongest moments and turn them into captioned vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn. A clip pack of 5 clips starts from £195 per episode, 10 clips from £345, and monthly clips from £595 a month.

Can I get a free marketing check?

Yes. Book a free 20-minute call and we’ll look at where you are and suggest one or two things worth doing next. No obligation. Book a free check, or try our brand health check first.

How do I get started?

Send us a message using the contact form, or book a call at a time that suits you. We’ll talk through what you want your marketing to achieve and your budget, then suggest the right mix of services.
