Recording anywhere

Mobile studio and remote recording

Can't come to the studio? We can bring the studio to you, or record guests wherever they are in the world.

RØDE mobile studio

Our portable kit is built around the RØDECaster Pro II. We set it up at your office, event or chosen venue and run the session just as we would in the studio.

  • Four microphone inputs for up to four people in the room
  • Set up and sound-checked on location before you start
  • Recording monitored throughout

Remote recording with Riverside

When guests can't be in the same room, we record with Riverside. Guests join from a link in their web browser, and each person is recorded in high quality on their own device, so a patchy internet connection doesn't spoil the recording.

  • Guests join from a browser link
  • A separate audio and video track for each speaker
  • Edited and produced just like a studio session
