Behind the scenes: filming The Dokkcast at Hale House

The Dokkcast is Udokk's podcast. We filmed and recorded this episode at Hale House in London, as part of an ongoing season that we distribute across Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube and other podcast networks.

The setup

Every speaker had their own microphone and headphones, and a camera between the seats captured both sides of the conversation. Everything was set up and sound-checked before the guests sat down, so they could walk in and start talking.

What we did

  • Filming and audio recording on location
  • Production
  • Distribution across Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube and other podcast networks

One recording, every platform

Filming as well as recording means one session covers every format. The finished episode goes out as audio to podcast apps and as video on YouTube, from a single conversation.
