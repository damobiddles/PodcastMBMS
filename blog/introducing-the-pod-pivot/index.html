Introducing The Pod Pivot: a podcast for businesses that want to podcast properly

We’re launching a podcast of our own. The Pod Pivot is a short, sharp show for founders, marketers and business owners who are thinking about starting a podcast, or who already have one and aren’t getting what they hoped for from it.

I regularly talk to businesses that know a podcast could work for them but aren’t sure where to start. I also hear from people a few dozen episodes in, wondering why the downloads have stalled or why it feels like so much effort for so little return. The Pod Pivot is for both groups.

Short, sharp and useful

Episodes run for 25 to 35 minutes. That’s long enough to get into something properly, and short enough to fit into a commute, a lunch break or a walk between meetings. No hour-long warm-ups and no padding: each episode should leave you with something you can use.

Two kinds of episode

  • Guest conversations. Local business people and people from across the podcast and marketing industry, talking about what they’ve built, what they’ve learned and how they use audio and video to reach people.
  • Solo and duo breakdowns. Shorter, focused episodes where we pick apart what works and what doesn’t, from choosing a format and booking guests to editing, clips and getting a show found.

Whether you’ve not recorded a word yet or you’re trying to rescue a show that’s lost its way, the aim is the same: help you make a podcast that’s worth your time and your listeners’ time.

Every episode is a showreel

The Pod Pivot is made with the same equipment, setup and editing process we use for our clients. Some episodes will be recorded in the studio; others will be out and about, with our mobile kit and outdoor mics, wherever the conversation makes most sense. Either way, every episode doubles as a showreel. If you’ve ever wondered what an MBMS-produced podcast sounds and looks like, in a studio or on location, you won’t have to take our word for it: just press play.

It also keeps us honest. It’s one thing to give advice about sound, structure and promotion; it’s another to follow it, in public, on every episode of our own show.

Listen now

The Pod Pivot is live. Press play below, or follow the show on Spotify or Apple Podcasts so you don’t miss an episode. I’ll share new episodes here on the blog as they’re released, along with a few notes on how each one was put together.

Run a local business with a story to tell, or thinking about a podcast of your own? I’d love to hear from you, whether that’s as a guest on The Pod Pivot or to talk about your own show.
