The full podcast package: show, website, social and email, done for you

A podcast on its own is a good start. A podcast with a proper home, a recognisable look, active social channels and an email list behind it is a marketing channel. Our full podcast package covers all of it, so you can focus on the conversation.

What’s in the full package

  1. Podcast productionPlanning each episode around a goal, recording in our London studio, at your office or remotely, editing, show notes and publishing to Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube.
  2. A podcast websiteA home for the show with a page for every episode, players, show notes and transcripts, plus enquiry and sign-up forms that feed straight into your inbox or CRM, such as HubSpot. Built to be found in search, so every episode keeps bringing people in.
  3. Brand and creative assetsCover art in square, widescreen and vertical formats, social banners and profile pictures, clip and quote templates, and a media kit guests and partners can use.
  4. Social media managementCaptioned clips, LinkedIn posts and a four-week posting plan for every episode, published and managed on your channels.
  5. Email campaignsA launch email, a branded newsletter template and an email for every new episode, so your list hears about the show first.

Example 1: The Dokkcast for Udokk

The Dokkcast is Udokk’s podcast. We record it at our London studio and on location at their office, produce every episode and publish it everywhere.

We also built the show’s website, thedokkcast.com, with a page for every episode. Each release gets its own short clips for social, all pointing back to the episode page, so listeners, search engines and social followers all end up in the same place.

Example 2: MBMS Podcast Studio and The Podcast Pivot

We do the same for ourselves. For our own business and our podcast, The Podcast Pivot, we’ve built:

  • This website. Services and prices, a blog, enquiry forms, a playbook waitlist and a free downloadable checklist.
  • A brand and media kit. Logos, LinkedIn banners, profile pictures, colours and fonts, all in our media kit.
  • Podcast artwork in every format. Square cover art for the podcast apps, widescreen for YouTube and vertical for Stories and Reels.
  • Social content for every episode. Show notes, chapters, LinkedIn posts for our personal and company pages, and clips.

If you like what you see here, this is what we’d build for you. Read the full story in our MBMS case study.

Who it’s for

  • Businesses launching a podcast that want it done properly from day one
  • Teams with a show that’s running, but no website, social plan or email behind it
  • Marketing teams who want the podcast to feed their wider campaigns without adding to their workload
