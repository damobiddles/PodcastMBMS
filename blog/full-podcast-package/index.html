The full podcast package: planned, made, shared and grown for you

A podcast on its own is a good start. A podcast that’s planned around a goal, published everywhere people listen and watch, and cut into shorts that keep finding new people is a marketing channel. Our full podcast package covers all of it, so you can focus on the conversation.

What’s in the full package

  1. Plan and coachA show format built around what the podcast should do for your business, episode and guest plans, and honest feedback after every recording.
  2. RecordIn our London studio at Great Portland Street, at your office or venue with our mobile kit, or remotely with Riverside.
  3. Edit and transcribeAudio and video editing, an edited transcript, show notes and episode descriptions.
  4. DistributeEvery episode in video and audio on YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music and the rest, with titles and descriptions written for each platform.
  5. Shorts and a monthly calendarClips, captions and covers from every episode using our own app, Offcuts, laid out as a month of posts in Post Desk, ready for you to approve.
  6. YouTube channel growthTitles, thumbnails, playlists and Shorts that lead people back to the full episode, led by Peter. Organic growth, no paid ads.

Example 1: The Dokkcast for Udokk

The Dokkcast is Udokk’s podcast. We record it at our London studio and on location at their office, edit every episode and publish it everywhere.

Each release gets its own short clips for social, made with Offcuts, so one recording turns into a month of posts that all point back to the full episode. Read more in our Dokkcast story.

Example 2: The Podcast Pivot

We do the same for ourselves. For our own podcast, The Podcast Pivot, we make:

  • Podcast artwork in every format. Square cover art for the podcast apps, widescreen for YouTube and vertical for Stories and Reels, all in our media kit.
  • Show notes and chapters. So listeners can find the bit they want, and search engines can understand every episode.
  • Clips for every episode. Short, captioned moments for YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok.

If you like what you see, this is what we’d do for your show. See more in our work.

Who it’s for

  • Businesses launching a podcast that want it done properly from day one
  • Teams with a show that’s running, but no time to clip it, post it or grow its YouTube channel
  • Marketing teams who want the podcast to feed their wider plans without adding to their workload
