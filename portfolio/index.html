Career

Where I’ve worked

  1. 2023 – 2026

    Arco Cyber Cybersecurity software and services scale-up, acquired by Sophos

    Chief Marketing Officer, 2023 – 2025 · Retained Marketing and Communications Consultant, 2025 – 2026

    • Led marketing, brand, communications, content and demand generation as the senior marketing leader, working closely with Sales, Product and the leadership team.
    • Built the commercial story, market positioning and executive thought leadership that strengthened the company’s authority ahead of the acquisition.
    • Made complex cybersecurity services compelling for customers, partners and senior decision-makers through campaigns, sector content, digital, SEO and sales enablement tied to pipeline.
    • Led a team of four plus specialist agencies, and owned the marketing budget, performance reporting and board updates as the business grew from start-up to scale-up.
    • After the Sophos acquisition, kept campaigns, positioning, and leadership, customer and commercial communications running through the integration.

  2. 2022 – 2023

    SSP Worldwide International insurance technology and B2B software provider

    Senior Marketing Manager

    • Directed marketing communications, content strategy, go-to-market messaging and brand governance across the UK, EMEA and APAC.
    • Built and mentored a 16-person marketing team across three regions, managing a budget for each and bringing in clearer ownership, priorities and delivery.
    • Lined campaigns up with revenue priorities and product roadmaps, working with Sales, Product and senior leadership.
    • Developed messaging and assets for complex products and many stakeholder groups, and supported major industry events.

  3. 2019 – 2022

    White Bullet Solutions Anti-piracy software covering ad fraud, brand safety and digital regulation

    Head of Marketing and Communications

    • Led global marketing, communications, thought leadership and product messaging in a technical, highly regulated market.
    • Built authority through expert-led content, media work and communications for C-suite buyers, industry partners and policy makers.
    • Turned complex technology, regulation and intellectual property issues into clear stories across digital, campaigns, events and partnerships.

  4. 2012 – 2019

    D&B Digital / Tee Tree Group

    Director of Digital Marketing

    • Directed content and digital strategy across enterprise technology, healthcare and retail, leading integrated campaigns, digital communications and ecommerce.
    • Managed teams, budgets, clients and partners, including high-compliance work for Pfizer, Baxter Healthcare and Huntsworth Health.

  5. 2009 – 2012

    The Circle Agency

    Head of Digital

    • Led digital strategy and video-led campaigns for Electronic Arts, Dell, Pioneer and Symantec, managing creative and technical teams.
    • Recognised by .NET Magazine for innovation in interactive video and branded digital experiences.
