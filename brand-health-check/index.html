Brand health check

Ten quick questions, about two minutes. Answer honestly and you’ll get a simple score and the areas we’d look at first. It’s a self-check based on your own answers, not a full audit, and nothing you tick is saved or sent anywhere.

  1. You can explain what you do in one sentence that a customer would repeat.
  2. Your website, social profiles and sales materials all say the same thing.
  3. You know who your best customers are, and why they choose you.
  4. Your website makes it obvious what a visitor should do next.
  5. People find you when they search for what you do.
  6. You share useful content regularly, not just news and announcements.
  7. When you make something good, you reuse it across channels instead of posting it once.
  8. Other people talk about you: press, podcasts, partners or reviews.
  9. You know which marketing activity actually brings in enquiries.
  10. You have a simple marketing plan for the next three months.
