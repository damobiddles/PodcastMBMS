Get your free podcast check

Paste a link to your show and tell us what you want it to do. In about a minute you’ll get a plain-English read of how your show comes across to a new listener: your description, your episode titles and show notes, your publishing rhythm, three quick wins for the next episode and one bigger opportunity. We’ll email you a copy too.

  • Free, no card, no obligation
  • Works with Apple Podcasts, RSS feeds and YouTube
  • Written for podcasters, not producers

We use your details only to send your check and, if you ticked the second box, to follow up once. We never add you to a mailing list, and every email has a link to delete your details. See our privacy notice.
