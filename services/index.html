New · For YouTube podcasters

Clips from your YouTube podcast

Already publishing long-form episodes on YouTube? We find the strongest moments in each one and turn them into captioned, ready-to-post short clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn. No recording needed: just send us the episode.

What's included

  • Watching the full episode and choosing the moments most likely to perform
  • Cutting each clip to vertical 9:16, with a strong opening line
  • Styled, word-by-word captions in your colours and fonts
  • A hook title, description and hashtags for each clip
  • Square versions for LinkedIn on request
  • One round of changes

How it works

  1. Send us the episode. A YouTube link works; the original recording files give sharper clips.
  2. We pick and cut the moments. Strong opinions, surprising facts, stories with a payoff and the bits people argue about in the comments.
  3. You review and post. Clips arrive ready to upload, usually within five working days. We can schedule them for you too.
