Already on YouTube?

Clips from your YouTube podcast

Already publishing long-form episodes on YouTube? We find the strongest moments in each one and turn them into captioned, ready-to-post short clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn. No recording needed: just send us the episode.

What's included

  • Watching the full episode and choosing the moments most likely to perform
  • Cutting each clip to vertical 9:16, with a strong opening line
  • Styled, word-by-word captions in your colours and fonts
  • A hook title, description and hashtags for each clip
  • Square versions for LinkedIn on request
  • One round of changes

How it works

  1. Send us the episode. A YouTube link works; the original recording files give sharper clips.
  2. We pick and cut the moments. Strong opinions, surprising facts, stories with a payoff and the bits people argue about in the comments.
  3. You review and post. Clips arrive ready to upload, usually within five working days. We can schedule them for you too.

Podcast FAQ

Podcast questions we're often asked

Straight answers on prices, recording, distribution and YouTube. Anything else, just ask.

How much does it cost to record a podcast in London?

A recording session costs £250 for up to 90 minutes. That includes preparing the studio, a sound check, monitoring the recording and supplying the raw audio files afterwards. Our prices are guidelines, and we're happy to be flexible depending on the work. See worked examples in our guide to podcast costs.

How much does podcast editing cost?

Audio editing starts from £175 per episode, and audio and video editing from £350 per episode. Edited transcripts start from £85 per episode, and show notes and episode descriptions from £95 per episode.

Do you offer a complete podcast package?

Yes. A complete package covering recording, editing, show notes, distribution and three social clips starts from around £950 per episode. For regular customers we can build a monthly package based on the number of episodes and campaign assets you need.

Where do you record podcasts?

We're based in Marlow, Buckinghamshire, and record at a choice of venues in London, including Great Portland Street Studio. We can also bring our RØDE mobile studio to your office, event or chosen venue in Marlow, the Thames Valley, London or further afield, or record guests remotely.

Can you record podcast guests remotely?

Yes. We record remote guests with Riverside. Guests join from a link in their web browser, and each person is recorded in high quality on their own device, so a patchy internet connection doesn't spoil the recording. Each speaker gets a separate audio and video track for editing.

Can you make short clips from my existing YouTube podcast?

Yes. Send us an episode, as a YouTube link or the original recording files, and we'll choose the strongest moments and turn them into captioned vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn. A clip pack of 5 clips starts from £195 per episode, 10 clips from £345, and monthly clips from £595 a month.

Can you turn podcast episodes into social media content?

Yes. We cut short clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn with our own app, Offcuts, write a caption for each platform, and hand over a month of posts in Post Desk, ready to post.

Which platforms can you publish my podcast to?

Every major platform, in video and audio: YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music and the other main podcast apps. We upload, schedule and publish each episode for you.

Can you grow our podcast’s YouTube channel?

Yes. Peter Bidmead leads our YouTube work: titles, thumbnails, descriptions, playlists and Shorts that lead back to full episodes, plus a regular look at what is working. We grow channels organically, without paid ads.

Tell us about your podcast, or the one you’re planning. Book a free 20-minute podcast chat and we’ll suggest the right mix, with a clear price.
