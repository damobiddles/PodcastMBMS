Our approach

How we think about marketing

Most businesses don't need more channels. They need something worth saying, said clearly, in the places their customers already look. Our method starts there. We find what's worth saying, shape it into a clear brand, share it across the right channels, then measure what it does for the business and improve it.

The method

One conversation, four steps

Everything we do starts with a good conversation: with you, your team, your customers or the experts in your field. That one conversation becomes the raw material for your brand, your channels and your growth.

  1. Listen

    Find what's worth saying

    We talk to you, and often to your customers, to find the problems you solve, the words people actually use and the stories only you can tell. Sometimes that conversation is recorded as a podcast or video, so nothing is wasted.

    • Podcasts and content

  2. Shape

    Turn it into a clear brand

    We pull out a simple position and a few plain lines that explain what you do and who it's for, then give it a look that fits. Everything after this speaks with the same voice.

    • Brand strategy & identity
    • Creative & web design

  3. Share

    Put it where your customers are

    One conversation becomes clips, posts, articles, emails, pages and pitches, planned across the month and sent to the channels that matter for your customers, not every channel going.

    • Social media & community
    • Digital & performance marketing
    • PR & influencer outreach

  4. Learn

    Measure what it does

    We agree a few measures that matter to the business at the start, such as enquiries, sign-ups or sales conversations, check them as the work goes out and use what we learn to plan the next conversation.

    • Data & growth analysis

Then back to step 1: what we learn shapes the next conversation, so the work gets sharper every month.

Our principles

What we believe, and how it shows up in the work

Start with the customer, not the channel

Before we talk about TikTok or ads, we want to know who you're trying to reach and what they need to hear. The channels follow from that.

Say less, more clearly

If people can't repeat what you do after one read, the message isn't finished. We cut until it's simple, then stop.

Make once, use everywhere

Good material is expensive to make. We plan every piece so it can be reused across your channels, instead of being posted once and forgotten.

Measure what the business cares about

Likes and views are useful signals, not the goal. We agree the few numbers that tell you whether the marketing is paying its way.

Plain English, always

No jargon in our plans, our reports or your marketing. If we can't explain something simply, we haven't understood it yet.

Honest advice, even when it's "don't"

If a channel, a campaign or a podcast isn't right for you yet, we'll say so, and suggest what would work better.
