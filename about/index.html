About us

Podcasts made by marketers

MBMS Podcast Studio is a podcast studio with a marketing background, based in Marlow, Buckinghamshire, and recording in London, on location and remotely. We don’t just record and edit your episodes: we help you shape the idea, plan the guests and turn every conversation into content that reaches the right people.

Founder

Damien Bidmead

Damien has spent more than 15 years in marketing, leading brand, content and demand generation for B2B technology, SaaS, cybersecurity, fintech and health businesses. He founded MBMS Podcast Studio to bring that experience to podcasting: every show starts with who you want to reach and what you want it to achieve, not just with a microphone.

Before MBMS, Damien was Head of Marketing Communications at Arco Cyber, where he grew lead generation to pipeline by 30% and led content and positioning through the company’s acquisition. Earlier roles include Senior Marketing Manager at SSP Worldwide, Head of Marketing Communications at White Bullet, Director of Digital Marketing at Tee Tree Group and Head of Digital Marketing at The Circle Agency, delivering campaigns for brands including Dell, Electronic Arts and Symantec.

He studied Film and English, which is where the love of video and storytelling started, and he’s comfortable on both sides of the camera: planning, filming, editing and writing. Outside work, Damien coaches Brazilian Jiu Jitsu with the Roger Gracie Academy, which is how Jiu Jitsu Fieldnotes ended up on our blog.
