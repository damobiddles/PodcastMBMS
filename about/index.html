About us

A small team of experienced marketers

Meadow Brand Marketing Services (MBMS) is a boutique marketing firm based in Marlow, Buckinghamshire. We help growing businesses work out what to say, say it clearly and get it in front of the right people. Content runs through everything we do: one good conversation becomes the brand, the posts, the pages and the campaigns.

Who you’ll work with

The team

Founder

Damien Bidmead

More than 15 years in marketing, agency-side and in-house, leading brand, content and demand generation. Most recently Chief Marketing Officer at Arco Cyber from 2023 to 2025, where he built the positioning and thought leadership that carried the company through its acquisition by Sophos, then stayed on to lead marketing through the integration.

Along the way: a 30% increase in inbound leads, a 20% lift in brand awareness, a 15% lower cost of winning each customer, and content made around 40% faster with AI-enabled workflows.

Earlier roles include SSP Worldwide, White Bullet, Tee Tree Group and The Circle Agency, with work for brands including Dell, Electronic Arts, Symantec, Pioneer, Pfizer, Baxter Healthcare and Huntsworth Health.

He completed Tech Nation Cyber’s Digital Marketing for Technology Scale-ups programme and is a certified Mental Health First Aider.

Sectors we know

Experience across nine sectors

Between us, we’ve worked in these sectors agency-side and in-house. Here are the challenges we know well, so you don’t have to explain them from scratch.

B2B software (SaaS)

  • Long sales cycles with several people involved in every decision
  • Websites that talk about features instead of the problems they solve
  • Sales teams that need content they can actually send

Cybersecurity

  • Technical buyers who are wary of being sold to
  • A crowded market where every vendor sounds the same
  • Building trust with expertise rather than fear

Insurance technology

  • Complex products sold to brokers, insurers and partners
  • Relationships and reputation that matter as much as features
  • Saying something useful in a careful, regulated industry

Healthcare

  • Explaining evidence simply without overpromising
  • Content that has to be checked and signed off by experts
  • Audiences who want trusted sources, not hype

Retail

  • Seasonal peaks and tight campaign deadlines
  • Standing out on more than price
  • Keeping customers coming back after the first purchase

Video games

  • Launches built around release dates and announcements
  • Communities that spot anything that isn’t genuine
  • Keeping momentum going between releases

Fintech

  • Earning trust when people are handing over their money
  • Making financial products easy to understand
  • Marketing inside strict financial promotion rules

Martech

  • Selling to marketers, who know every trick in the book
  • Standing out among hundreds of similar tools
  • Showing how a product fits into the tools people already use

FMCG

  • Winning attention on a crowded shelf and a busy feed
  • Keeping a brand consistent across retailers and markets
  • Launching new products fast without losing what people love

These describe the sectors we have experience in, not MBMS client work. See our work for projects we’ve delivered as MBMS, and example plans for how we’d approach yours.
